Generated by `node scripts/wcag-coverage.js` — do not edit by hand.

All 86 success criteria of WCAG 2.2, and how the native engine covers them.
"Partially automated" means our rules catch common failures but cannot prove conformance;
"Manual review" criteria need a human (most of the spec — true for every automated tool).
No criterion is listed as fully "Automated": every one of them ends in a question about
meaning that only a person can settle, even where a rule decides most real pages.
